# Oracle Fusion Financials – General Ledger (GL) Implementation Simulation

## End-to-End Enterprise Setup, Chart of Accounts, Journal Processing, Financial Reporting and Period Close

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a simulated end-to-end implementation of Oracle Fusion Financials General Ledger (GL) for a manufacturing organization.

The project covers key General Ledger functional concepts and activities, including Enterprise Setup, Chart of Accounts design, journal processing, financial reporting, reconciliation, and period-close activities.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate practical functional knowledge of Oracle Fusion Financials and the Record-to-Report (R2R) business process.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario

The project is based on a simulated manufacturing organization.

The organization requires a structured General Ledger setup to support:

- Financial transaction recording
- Account classification
- Journal processing
- Financial reporting
- Period-end reconciliation
- Trial Balance generation
- Profit and Loss reporting
- Balance Sheet reporting
- Month-end and period-close activities

---

# 🔧 Implementation Scope

## 1. Enterprise Setup

The project covers the design of an enterprise structure for the simulated organization.

Key areas include:

- Enterprise Structure
- Legal Entity
- Business Unit
- Ledger
- Functional Currency
- Accounting Calendar
- Accounting Method
- Primary Balancing Segment

---

## 2. Chart of Accounts Design

A five-segment Chart of Accounts structure was designed to support financial reporting and account analysis.

The segments include:

1. Company
2. Cost Center
3. Natural Account
4. Product
5. Future

The Chart of Accounts supports classification of financial accounts across:

- Assets
- Liabilities
- Revenue
- Expenses
- Equity

---

## 3. General Ledger and Journal Processing

The project demonstrates General Ledger journal processing and financial transaction recording.

Key activities include:

- Journal Entry Preparation
- Journal Posting
- Account Classification
- Financial Transaction Recording
- Period-Based Journal Processing
- Suspense Account Monitoring
- Intercompany Reconciliation
- Journal Validation

---

## 4. Financial Reporting

The project includes preparation and analysis of key financial reports.

Reports covered include:

- Trial Balance
- Profit and Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet

These reports are used to validate financial balances and demonstrate how General Ledger transactions are reflected in financial statements.

---

## 5. Record-to-Report (R2R) Process

The project demonstrates concepts related to the Record-to-Report business cycle.

Activities include:

- Recording financial transactions
- Journal processing
- Posting transactions to General Ledger
- Account reconciliation
- Trial Balance validation
- Financial reporting
- Period-end activities

---

## 6. Period Close Activities

A simulated period-close process was performed covering:

- Review of journal entries
- Journal posting validation
- Account reconciliation
- Suspense account review
- Intercompany reconciliation
- Trial Balance validation
- Profit and Loss reporting
- Balance Sheet validation
- Period status management
